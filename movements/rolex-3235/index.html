Rolex 3235 accuracy

±2 s/day

−2/+2 s/day after casing (Superlative Chronometer). The movement is first COSC-certified.

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20260206195940/https://www.rolex.com/en-us/watches/datejust/m126334-0014. Source type: maker (archive). Read 2026-11-24.

Is my watch normal?

If your Rolex 3235 watch runs inside this range, it is running as the maker intends. A new watch can take some days to settle.

How to measure it

  1. Open Regulator, add your watch and choose Rolex 3235 as the movement.
  2. Tap when the seconds hand reaches 12.
  3. Do it again after 24 hours. Regulator gives the rate in seconds a day and compares it with this range.
