Seiko 7S26 accuracy

No published spec

Seiko's 7S26 manual gives no daily rate. Other sites quote values that do not agree, so Regulator shows none.

Source: https://www.seikowatches.com/us-en/-/media/Files/Common/Seiko/instructions/English/7/7S26/7S26.pdf. Source type: maker (no value). Read 2026-11-24.

Is my watch normal?

We found no rate for this movement from its maker or a standards body, so Regulator does not judge it. Regulator still measures the rate, and you can enter your own range.

How to measure it

  1. Open Regulator, add your watch and choose Seiko 7S26 as the movement.
  2. Tap when the seconds hand reaches 12.
  3. Do it again after 24 hours. Regulator gives the rate in seconds a day.
