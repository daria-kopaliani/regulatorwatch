How do I measure?

Open Measure Now. Tap the dial at the moment your seconds hand reaches 12. Check the minute and save. Do it again after 24 hours.

Why does the rate need two readings?

One reading tells you how far the watch is from the right time. The rate is how fast that distance changes, so it needs a second reading later. 24 hours apart gives about ±0.6 s/day precision from tap timing.

I set the time on my watch. What now?

Tap I Set My Watch. It starts a new run, so the old readings do not spoil the new rate.

Where does the reference time come from?

From time.apple.com. If it is not reachable, Regulator uses the iPhone clock. Keep Settings › General › Date & Time › Set Automatically on.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase.
