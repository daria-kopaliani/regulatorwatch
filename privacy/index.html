Privacy policy

Updated 2026-11-24.

Regulator does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer has no server and does not receive your watches or readings.

Where your data is stored

Regulator saves your watches and readings in its own storage on your iPhone.

Your iPhone backups can include this data, as they include the data of most apps. If iCloud Backup is on, your iCloud backup contains a copy. If you back up your iPhone to a computer, that backup contains a copy. You control these backups in the settings of your iPhone. You can also turn off iCloud Backup for Regulator and keep it on for other apps. Apple or your computer keeps the backups. The developer cannot see them.

If you restore your iPhone from a backup that contains this data, your watches and readings come back.

CSV files that you share

With Regulator Pro, you can export the readings of a watch as a CSV file. For the export, Regulator writes the file in a temporary folder on your iPhone. Then the share sheet opens. The file goes only to the place that you choose, for example Files, Mail or Messages. Regulator does not send it anywhere by itself.

Network connections

To get the reference time, the app sends a standard time request (SNTP) to time.apple.com. The request carries no information about you. If no valid answer comes back, the app uses the iPhone clock.

Apple handles purchases. The app gets the details of Regulator Pro from the App Store, and each purchase and restore goes through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.
