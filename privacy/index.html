Privacy policy

Updated 2026-11-24.

Regulator does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Your watches and readings are stored on your iPhone only. If you export a CSV file, you choose where it goes.

To get the reference time, the app sends a standard time request (SNTP) to time.apple.com. The request carries no information about you.

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.
